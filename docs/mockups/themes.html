The picker  Super + Shift + T, or "Change theme" in the launcher. It opens on the current theme; arrows move, Enter applies. Bar, borders, launcher, terminals, notifications, lock screen, GTK accent and wallpaper change together in under a second, no logout. Login and boot screens stay Dusk.
